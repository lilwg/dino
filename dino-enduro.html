How it works

DinoEnduro recreates the 1983 Activision original: a curving desert road, day/night/snow cycles, and a 200-car-per-day target. The road vanishing point drifts left and right like a gentle curve. The AI uses a two-second lookahead to find the least-congested lane.

Tap Viz to see per-lane danger overlaid directly on the road surface.
