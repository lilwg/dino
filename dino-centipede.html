How it works

DinoCentipede recreates the 1981 Atari classic. A multi-segment centipede snakes down through a mushroom field; shooting a segment turns it into a mushroom and splits the chain. Spiders (pterodactyls) zigzag through the player zone, scorpions (cacti) poison mushrooms causing the centipede to dive, and fleas drop from above when the player area runs low on mushrooms.

The AI uses a priority system: dodge the spider when it gets dangerously close, otherwise target the nearest centipede segment. It aligns horizontally with the target and fires when it has a clear vertical shot. When the spider is nearby, the AI moves to maximize distance. Enable Viz to see the targeting line (green to the current target), danger zones (red circles around the spider), and the AI decision label.
