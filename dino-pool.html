DinoPool

8-Ball Pool — sink all your balls, then the 8-ball

Click & drag to aim & set power  |  R restart

How it works

Classic 8-ball pool with dino theming. Solids (T-Rex eggs) and stripes (Pterodactyl eggs) are assigned after the first ball is pocketed. Sink all your group, then pocket the 8-ball to win. Scratching (pocketing the cue ball) gives your opponent ball-in-hand.

Click and drag away from the cue ball to aim your shot. The farther you drag, the more power. Release to shoot. In vs AI mode, the AI opponent uses shot simulation to find the best pocket angle.
