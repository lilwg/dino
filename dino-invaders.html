How it works

The AI follows a simple priority system: (1) dodge enemy bullets that will hit you, (2) shoot the UFO if it’s on screen, (3) move toward and shoot the lowest alien, (4) fire opportunistically at any alien you pass while moving. No simulation or lookahead — just reactive rules that scan the board each frame.

Tap Viz to watch the AI think. A dashed line shows what the AI is pursuing — blue for the lowest alien, orange for the UFO. A crosshair marks the target position. Red “DODGE” text appears when evading an incoming bullet. The badge below the player shows the chosen action.
