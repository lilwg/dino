Neural Network (MLP with Backpropagation)

A 2-layer neural network (10 → 64 → 3) trained via backpropagation. Click Train to collect 40k frames of gameplay from the sim AI (headless, takes a few seconds), then train the network with class-balanced mini-batch SGD for 200 epochs. Once trained, click Play to watch the network play.

k-Nearest Neighbors

No training at all — just stores examples from the sim AI and classifies new states by majority vote of the k closest neighbors. Click Collect to gather data, then Play to watch it run.

Neuroevolution (Evolutionary RL)

No teacher, no gradients — evolves a small neural network (10→16→3) by natural selection. A population of 50 agents play the game; the top 10 survive and produce mutated offspring. Each generation evaluates every genome across 3 episodes at random speeds. Mutation rate (σ) decays over time to refine the best solutions. Click Start to evolve, then Play to watch the fittest genome.

How it works

The AI uses forward simulation to decide when to jump or duck. Each frame, it clones the current game state and simulates the next 80 frames for three scenarios: keep running, jump now, or duck now. If running straight will collide with an obstacle, it picks whichever alternative keeps the dinosaur alive.

Tap Viz to watch the AI think. A cloud of simulated paths appears ahead of the dinosaur — blue for jumping, orange for ducking, dark for running. The thick line is the chosen path; fainter lines are alternatives the AI evaluated. A badge above the dino shows the current decision.
