DinoSeaquest

Rescue baby dinos from the prehistoric sea

Arrows move  |  Space shoot (one shot)  |  Surface to rescue  |  R restart

How it works

DinoSeaquest recreates the 1983 Activision original: one bullet at a time fired in your facing direction, 6-diver capacity, a depleting oxygen supply, and escalating waves of pterodactyl fish. Surface with divers to score 150 pts each; surface with none to just refill oxygen. An enemy sub patrols the surface when you have divers aboard.

Tap Viz to see the AI's target diver circled in green, dangerous fish in red, and a dashed line to the surface when the AI plans to ascend.
