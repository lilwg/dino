How it works

DinoBeamRider recreates the 1982 Activision original: one bullet at a time, sector-by-sector waves of pterodactyls and blocker cacti, and a sentinel boss every three sectors. The AI measures incoming threat per lane, moves toward the safest firing position, and waits for the bullet to clear before reloading.

Tap Viz to see per-lane threat scores overlaid on the grid and a dashed line tracing the bullet path.
