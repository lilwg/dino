How it works

DinoQbert recreates the 1982 Gottlieb arcade original: hop-based movement, one jump per keypress with a 150ms arc animation, and the classic @!#?@! swear bubble on death. Coily spawns as an egg, bounces randomly down the pyramid, then hatches and chases the player. Dark balls bounce down and kill on contact; light balls are safe to catch for 100 pts. Slick (round 4+) hops down uncoloring cubes. Two transport discs at variable positions let Q*bert escape and lure Coily to his death.

Tap Viz to reveal the AI’s plan: a dashed line traces the BFS path to its next target, with faint dots marking the rest of its queued tour. The AI forward-simulates Coily’s greedy chase 3 steps ahead to build both immediate and predicted danger maps, then plans a danger-weighted nearest-neighbour tour over all uncolored cubes. BFS pathfinding routes around dangerous tiles. Every move is scored by a multi-criteria evaluator: path progress, cube productivity, safety margin, escape-route count, and 2-step lookahead. It intercepts nearby Slicks for bonus points, and triggers disc escapes earlier—when cornered or when Coily closes to 3 hops—even maneuvering toward a disc edge when not already on one.
