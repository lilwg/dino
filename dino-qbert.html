How it works

DinoQbert recreates the 1982 Gottlieb arcade original: hop-based movement, one jump per keypress with a 150ms arc animation, and the classic @!#?@! swear bubble on death. Coily spawns as an egg, bounces randomly down the pyramid, then hatches and chases the player. Slick hops down uncoloring cubes. Two teleport discs on each side let Q*bert escape and kill Coily for a bonus.

Tap Viz to see the AI’s planned BFS path drawn across the pyramid. The AI avoids enemy-adjacent cubes and will dive for a disc if Coily gets close.
