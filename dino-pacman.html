How it works

The AI uses BFS flood-fill to compute shortest distances from the T-Rex to every reachable tile. Each ghost projects a danger zone—tiles within a few steps are penalized based on proximity and ghost mode. When powered up, the AI flips strategy: it chases frightened ghosts using BFS, prioritizing the nearest vulnerable target for maximum combo score (200→400→800→1600).

In normal mode, the AI scores every reachable dot by combining its BFS distance with a ghost-danger penalty. Power pellets get a strategic bonus when ghosts are nearby—eating one at the right moment can clear a cluster of ghosts. The AI also avoids dead-end corridors when ghosts are close. Tap Viz to see ghost danger zones (red halos), the AI’s planned path (green dotted line), target highlights, and each ghost’s target tile (colored squares).
