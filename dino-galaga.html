How it works

DinoGalaga recreates the 1981 Namco classic. Pterodactyl enemies fly in from off-screen in sweeping formation patterns, then settle into a grid at the top. Once in formation they periodically dive-bomb the player in curved swooping paths, firing bullets. Boss enemies (large cacti) require two hits to destroy and can capture your ship — recapture it for dual-fire power. Every few stages a Challenging Stage sends waves of non-attacking enemies for bonus points.

The AI uses a rule-based targeting system: it identifies the lowest and nearest enemy, moves to align horizontally, and fires when lined up. It continuously scans for incoming enemy bullets and dodges laterally when threatened. Enable Viz to see the AI’s targeting line (green), danger zones around enemy bullets (red circles), and the current target highlight.
