How it works

The AI evaluates every possible placement for the current piece — all rotations and all horizontal positions — and scores each one using four classic Tetris heuristics: aggregate height (lower is better), completed lines (more is better), holes (fewer is better), and bumpiness (smoother is better). The weights are tuned to strongly penalize holes and height while rewarding line clears.

Tap Viz to watch the AI think. A green ghost outline shows the target placement. Column heights are drawn as bars along the bottom, holes are marked with red dots, and the top three candidate placements are shown as faint numbered outlines with their scores.
