How it works

The AI uses a lane-by-lane gap analysis. For traffic lanes, it projects vehicle positions forward and finds windows where the player can safely hop through. For river lanes, it identifies the nearest log or non-diving turtle group and times its jump to land on a safe platform. It always prefers forward progress but will move sideways or wait when no safe forward path exists.

Tap Viz to see the AI’s thinking: green highlights show safe zones in each lane, red zones mark danger areas, and a dashed line traces the planned path. Yellow circles mark target landing spots on logs and turtles. The AI scores each possible move (up, down, left, right, wait) and picks the highest-scoring option, balancing safety with forward progress and timer pressure.
